Why enterprises must act now

The threat is already here. Quantum is just the deadline.

Traffic recorded today can be read once a cryptographically relevant quantum computer exists. The algorithms that replace RSA and elliptic curves are standardised, and the deadlines are published.

Harvest now, decrypt later

Encrypted traffic captured today can be decrypted after Q-Day. Data with a long secrecy lifetime is already exposed.

Regulatory deadlines are set

NIST IR 8547 deprecates quantum-vulnerable public-key algorithms and then disallows them. NSA CNSA 2.0 sets dates per system type.

RSA/ECC 112-bit: deprecated 2030 · disallowed 2035

Certificates outlive plans

Roots, VPN gateways and backup archives stay in service for a decade. What you issue or encrypt now must survive the transition.

Nobody knows the inventory

Most teams cannot say which endpoints still negotiate classical key exchange. Migration starts with measuring it.

Hybrid is the bridge to post-quantum

Every product combines a classical algorithm with a post-quantum one, so security never drops below what you have today.

  • X25519 + ML-KEM-768 for TLS 1.3 and IKEv2 key exchange
  • ML-DSA certificates from your own CA, with SLH-DSA roots available
  • Crypto-agility: moving to CNSA 2.0 is a policy change, not a rewrite
  • Evidence for auditors, mapped to NIST IR 8547 and CNSA 2.0
