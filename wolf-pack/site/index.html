Finds every piece of cryptography in your software, tells you which of it a quantum computer will break, and ranks what to replace first. One command, one standard file (a CBOM) at the end.

wolfpack hunt path\to\repo --tls api.example.com:443

  • Windows, macOS, Linux

Why hunt at all

Quantum computers are not here yet, but the clock has already started.

1 / THE THREAT

RSA and elliptic curves will fall

A large enough quantum computer breaks them with Shor's algorithm. NIST deprecates them after 2030 and disallows them after 2035.

2 / THE CATCH

Harvest now, decrypt later

Traffic recorded today can be read once that computer exists. Data that must stay secret for ten years is already exposed.

3 / THE FIRST STEP

You can't replace what you can't find

Crypto hides in code, configs, certificates, binaries and servers. Wolf Pack finds it, checks it, and writes it down in a standard CBOM.

  • Python, Java, Go, JS/TS, C/C++, C#, Rust
  • nginx, Apache, HAProxy, sshd, OpenSSL
  • Certificates and keys
  • Binaries, JARs, class files
  • Dependency manifests
  • Live TLS and SSH servers

    Switch a wolf off

    Every role can be left out of a hunt with --without. Leaving one out shows what it contributes. Pick a row to see which (file, algorithm) pairs the pack gets wrong without it.

    RecallPrecision

    Scored on 91 labelled (file, algorithm family) pairs in the development corpus. That corpus was written alongside the scanner, so these numbers show that each role is live. They are not a measure of real-world accuracy. That needs a blind, held-out benchmark, which is the next stage of the work. Propagation also recovers parameters such as RSA-1024 rather than RSA, which family-level scoring cannot see.

    Start hunting

    Python 3.11 or newer. The commands below are for PowerShell; on macOS and Linux use source .venv/bin/activate.

    # set up once
cd wolf-pack
py -m venv .venv
.\.venv\Scripts\Activate.ps1
pip install -e ".[dev]"

# hunt a repository, and optionally its live endpoints
wolfpack hunt C:\path\to\repo
wolfpack hunt C:\path\to\repo --tls api.example.com:443 --ssh bastion.example.com

# in CI: fail only on crypto added since main
wolfpack hunt . --baseline main-cbom.json --fail-on high

# leave a member of the pack out, or score every ablation
wolfpack hunt . --without den
wolfpack bench bench\corpus
