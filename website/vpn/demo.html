A revoked branch is refused

Two real Acxelin VPN controllers, headquarters and a branch, captured when this site was built. They agree their tunnel's keys over post-quantum mutual TLS; then the branch's certificate is revoked and it gets nothing more.

{{VPN_CAPTURE}}

How to read it

Frames 1 and 2 set the scene: a private authority issues both sites ML-DSA certificates, and headquarters starts its key agreement service. Frame 3 is the branch connecting: the key exchange is the hybrid group that was negotiated, and each side presented its ML-DSA certificate.

Frame 4 is what each side would load into strongSwan. The key values are never shown or logged; the build compares the two sides' keys and fails if they differ. strongSwan itself does not run while the site is built, so this run stops at the keys; the tunnels themselves are tested between network namespaces in the product's own test suite. Frames 5 to 7 are the revocation: the branch is refused at its next attempt, and headquarters cuts it off.
